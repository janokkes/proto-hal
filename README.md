# Proto-HAL

**Semantic Saturation and Task Drift in AI Agents**

Proto-HAL explores a potential failure mode in AI agents: an agent may gradually shift away from its assigned task when the task environment offers diminishing opportunities for learning, while exploration continues to be rewarded.

The central hypothesis is that semantic saturation can change the relative attractiveness of task-relevant and task-irrelevant actions. An agent may continue to behave coherently and actively while becoming less responsive to its original objective.

This is not necessarily reward hacking or intentional power-seeking. Task drift may emerge from the interaction between a relatively fixed incentive for information gain and a declining amount of new information available within the assigned task.

## Research

The project develops theoretical models, operational definitions, and experimental approaches for studying semantic saturation, task drift, and objective stability.
